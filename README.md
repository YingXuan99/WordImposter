# Word Imposter
![Homepage Preview](/Word Imposter/src/images/homepage.png)


A social deduction party game for 3–10 players. One person is the imposter — they either have no word, or a slightly different one. Everyone gives clues. The group votes. Trust no one.

---

## How to Play

1. Choose a game mode and configure the number of players
2. Enter each player's name one at a time
3. Pass the phone around — each player taps to reveal their secret word privately
4. Everyone takes turns giving a one-word (or short) clue about their word
5. Discuss, argue, and accuse — then vote IRL on who the imposter is
6. Hit **Reveal Result** to unmask the imposter

---

## Game Modes

### Blind Mode
The imposter receives **no word at all**. They must bluff their way through the entire discussion without knowing what everyone else is talking about.

- **Agents** — vote out the imposter. If caught, the imposter gets one last chance: correctly guess the word to steal the win.
- **Imposter** — survive the vote. If voted out, guess the word correctly to win anyway.

### Sneaky Mode
The imposter receives a **similar but different word** (e.g. everyone has *Coffee*, the imposter has *Tea*). Nobody knows who the imposter is — not even the imposter themselves.

- **Agents** — find the player whose clues feel slightly off. Vote them out.
- **Imposter** — blend in. If you survive the vote, you win. No guessing required.

---

## Features

- 3–10 players
- Multi-imposter support (2 imposters for 6+ players)
- Animated sequential name entry with live queue
- Private per-player word reveal with tap-to-decrypt
- Live discussion timer with rotating suspicion feed
- Singapore-flavoured word list
